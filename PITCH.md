# Tartan Match

**Tartan Match** is a Tinder-style dating app built exclusively for Carnegie Mellon undergrads. It features a buttery-smooth swipe UI with drag physics, animated LIKE/NOPE stamps, and a celebratory match modal with floating hearts — all wrapped in CMU's signature tartan red and gold color palette. What sets it apart is its deep campus integration: profiles are organized by CMU's real colleges and majors, and every match comes with a suggested date spot pulled from beloved Pittsburgh locations like Phipps Conservatory, Craig St coffee shops, and The Fence. The 4-step onboarding flow captures your year, college, major, interests, and bio, creating rich profiles that go beyond surface-level swiping. Built with Next.js, shadcn/ui, Framer Motion, and Zustand, it's a polished, production-quality prototype that proves campus-specific dating can feel both personal and premium.
